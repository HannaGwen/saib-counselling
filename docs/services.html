My Approach

Individual counselling, built around you

I offer person-centred individual counselling — face-to-face in Llanelli or online via Microsoft Teams.

How I Can Help

People come to counselling for many different reasons

These are just examples of what might bring someone to counselling — not a fixed list. If something isn't mentioned here, please get in touch anyway.

  • Feeling anxious, worried or overwhelmed
  • Low mood or feeling down
  • Stress — from work, family or everyday life
  • Going through a difficult life change or transition
  • Feeling stuck, lost or unsure of yourself
  • Low self-esteem or lack of confidence
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Bereavement and loss
  • Feeling isolated or alone
  • Struggling to cope with a particular situation
  • Simply wanting somewhere confidential to talk things through

Fees

Straightforward, no surprises

Session Fees

£50 per 50-minute session. The initial 15-minute consultation is free, with no obligation to continue afterwards. There is currently no sliding scale available.

Payment

Payment is due on the day of your session. An invoice is sent with a Pay Now link and QR code, or you can pay by bank transfer. Cash is also accepted. No deposit is required to book.

Cancellations

I ask for at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or rearrange a session. A late cancellation (less than 48 hours' notice) is charged at £25, and a missed session with no notice is charged at £50.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a session cost?

Sessions are £50 for 50 minutes.

Do you offer a free initial consultation?

Yes — a free, no-obligation 15-minute conversation before you decide whether to go ahead.

Where are you based?

My counselling room is at Canolfan Antioch, Copperworks Road, Llanelli, Carmarthenshire, SA15 2NE.

Can I have sessions online?

Yes, online sessions are available via Microsoft Teams.

How do I pay?

By invoice with a Pay Now link and QR code, by bank transfer, or in cash.

When is payment due?

Payment is due on the day of your session.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 48 hours' notice. A late cancellation is £25, and a missed session with no notice is £50.

How many sessions will I need?

There's no fixed number. Some people start with six sessions and review from there; others prefer to take it one session at a time. We'll keep this under review together.

What if I don't know what to say?

That's completely normal, and nothing to worry about. There's no fixed agenda — we go at your pace, and it's fine to simply start wherever feels right.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. Sessions are confidential, in line with the BACP ethical framework, with narrow exceptions required by law.

Are you a member of a professional body?

Yes, I'm an Individual Member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP) and work within its ethical framework. I also hold Professional Indemnity and Public Liability insurance.

Do you accept insurance?

I currently work on a private-pay basis and am not registered with insurance providers, so sessions are self-funded.

Not sure where to start?

A free 15-minute consultation is a good first step — no obligation to continue afterwards.
