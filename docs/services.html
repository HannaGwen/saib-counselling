My Approach

Person-Centred Counselling

What to expect from our work together, how sessions are structured, and what it costs.

Person-centred counselling is based on the belief that you are the expert on your own experiences.

Rather than giving you instructions or telling you how you should feel, I aim to offer a relationship built around empathy, acceptance and genuineness.

This gives you space to explore what's happening for you, understand yourself more deeply and find your own way forward.

Counselling doesn't require you to arrive knowing exactly what you want to talk about. Sometimes you may simply know that things don't feel right. We can start there.

There is no expectation that you need to tell me everything immediately. We work at a pace that feels appropriate for you.

How I Can Help

People come to counselling for many different reasons. You may be experiencing:

  • Anxiety or persistent worry
  • Stress or feeling overwhelmed
  • Low mood
  • Bereavement, grief or loss
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Low self-esteem or confidence
  • Difficult life experiences
  • Changes or transitions in your life
  • Work-related stress
  • Feeling stuck, lost or unsure of yourself
  • Or simply wanting somewhere confidential to talk and make sense of how you're feeling

You don't need to fit neatly into one of these categories to come to counselling. If you're unsure whether counselling might be suitable for what you're experiencing, we can discuss this during the free initial consultation. Sessions are available face-to-face in Llanelli or online.

Working Together

Your First Counselling Session

Starting counselling can feel daunting, particularly if you've never experienced counselling before. You don't need to prepare anything or arrive knowing exactly what you're going to say.

Our first full session gives us more time to talk about what has brought you to counselling, what you might like from our work together and anything you feel is important for me to understand. Counselling is collaborative, and we'll regularly consider how you're finding the process.

There isn't a predetermined number of sessions that everyone needs. Some clients find it helpful to begin with an initial six sessions, after which we can review together how you're finding counselling and whether you'd like to continue. There is no requirement to have a set number of sessions, and this can be discussed throughout our work together.

Fees

Straightforward, no surprises

Counselling Sessions

£50 per 50-minute session. The initial 15-minute consultation is free.

Payment

Payment is due on the day of your session, following your appointment. No deposit is required.

Cancellations

I ask for at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or rearrange a session. A cancellation with less than 48 hours' notice is charged at 50% of the session fee (£25), and a missed session with no notice is charged at the full fee (£50).

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does counselling cost?

£50 per 50-minute session.

Do you offer an initial consultation?

Yes — a free 15-minute initial consultation, with no obligation to book a counselling session afterwards.

Where are sessions held?

Face-to-face sessions take place at SAIB Counselling, Canolfan Antioch, Copperworks Road, Llanelli, Carmarthenshire, SA15 2NE. Online sessions take place from a private space of your choosing.

How does online counselling work?

Online sessions are held via Microsoft Teams. A secure meeting link is emailed to you before your appointment. You'll need a reliable internet connection and a private space to talk.

How do I pay?

By invoice, with a secure bank payment (Pay Now link or QR code) or bank transfer. Bank payment is preferred, though cash is accepted if needed.

When do I need to pay?

Payment is due on the day of your session, following your appointment.

What happens if I need to cancel?

I ask for at least 48 hours' notice. A cancellation with less than 48 hours' notice is charged at 50% of the session fee (£25). A missed session with no notice is charged at the full fee (£50).

How many sessions will I need?

There isn't a fixed number. Some clients find it helpful to begin with an initial six sessions and then review together how things are going. There's no requirement to commit to a set number of sessions.

What if I don't know what to talk about?

That's completely okay — you don't need to arrive with everything worked out.

Is counselling confidential?

Yes. Sessions are confidential, with narrow exceptions required for safety or legal reasons, which I'll explain before we start.

Are you a member of a professional body?

Yes, I'm an Individual Member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP) and work within its ethical framework.

Do you accept insurance?

I currently work on a private-pay basis and am not registered with insurance providers, so sessions are self-funded.

Not sure where to start?

A free 15-minute consultation is a good first step — no obligation to continue afterwards.
