At SAIB, I aim to offer a warm, confidential and accepting environment where you can take that pause, explore what's important to you, and move forward at your own pace through person-centred counselling — face-to-face in Llanelli or online.

How counselling can help

Whatever has brought you here, there's space to talk it through

People come to counselling for many different reasons. You don't need to fit neatly into a category to get in touch.

🌊

Anxiety & Stress

Persistent worry, feeling overwhelmed, or the pressure of work and everyday life.

🌤️

Low Mood & Life Changes

Low mood, difficult life experiences, or feeling stuck, lost or unsure of yourself.

🕊️

Bereavement & Relationships

Grief and loss, relationship difficulties, or low self-esteem and confidence.

These are just some examples of what people bring to counselling — or you may simply want somewhere confidential to talk. Read more about my approach →
