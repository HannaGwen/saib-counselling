Welcome to SAIB

Life can sometimes feel overwhelming, leaving little space to stop, reflect and make sense of what you're experiencing.

At SAIB, I aim to offer a warm, confidential and accepting environment where you can take that pause, explore what's important to you, and move forward at your own pace through person-centred counselling.

Whether you're experiencing anxiety, low mood, bereavement or loss, relationship difficulties, stress, changes in your life, or simply feel that something isn't quite right, counselling can provide a space where you don't have to work through things alone.

Free Initial Consultation

Taking the First Step

Before arranging your first counselling session, I offer a free 15-minute initial consultation. This gives us an opportunity to touch base and briefly talk about what you're looking for from counselling. It also gives me an opportunity to explain a little about how I work and, importantly, gives you the chance to get a sense of whether you feel comfortable working with me. There is no obligation to book a counselling session afterwards.
