About SAIB Counselling

Care that meets you where you are

We started SAIB Counselling to make quality mental health care feel approachable — never clinical, never rushed.

Our Mission

Evidence-based care, delivered with heart

SAIB Counselling was founded on a simple belief: therapy should feel like a conversation with someone who truly gets it, backed by methods that are proven to work. We draw on cognitive-behavioural therapy, emotionally focused therapy, and trauma-informed care, tailoring each plan to the person in front of us.

Whether you're navigating anxiety, a relationship at a crossroads, grief, or simply feeling stuck, Jason is here to walk alongside you — not ahead of you.
