About SAIB Counselling

Hi, I'm Jason

SAIB is Welsh for “pause” — and that's exactly what I hope to offer: a space to stop, breathe, and find your own way forward.

About Me

A bit about my background

I hold a Foundation Degree in Counselling and have spent time working within the care and support sector, which has shaped the way I understand people and the challenges they face. I set up SAIB Counselling to bring that experience into a private, person-centred practice based in Llanelli.

I'm an Individual Member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP) and work within its ethical framework, and I hold Professional Indemnity and Public Liability insurance.

My Approach

Person-centred, at your pace

I work in a person-centred way, which means you are the expert in your own life — not me. Rather than following a fixed agenda or programme, I aim to create a warm, non-judgemental space where you can explore what matters to you, in your own time and at your own pace.

There's no set number of sessions you need to commit to. Some people find it helpful to start with six sessions and then review how things are going together; others prefer to take it one session at a time. Either way, the direction is always shaped by you.

Want to see if it feels like the right fit?

I offer a free 15-minute initial consultation, with no obligation to continue afterwards.
