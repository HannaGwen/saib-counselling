Privacy Notice

How your personal information is handled by SAIB Counselling.

Data Protection

SAIB Counselling is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Any personal information you share — whether by email, enquiry form, or during sessions — is kept confidential and is used only to provide counselling services to you. It is never sold, and it is never used for marketing purposes.

As a BACP Individual Member, I work within the BACP's ethical framework, which includes clear standards on confidentiality and record-keeping. There are narrow exceptions where information may need to be shared, such as where required by law or where there is a serious risk of harm.

A fuller Privacy Notice, covering what information is collected, how long it is retained, and your rights under UK data protection law, will be published here in due course. In the meantime, if you have any questions about how your information is handled, please get in touch at saibcounselling@outlook.com.
