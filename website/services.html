Our Services

Support for every stage of life

Each service below is offered in person and online, with flexible scheduling and sliding-scale options available on request.

🧠

Individual Therapy

One-on-one support for anxiety, depression, self-esteem, stress, and life transitions. Sessions are tailored to your goals, pace, and preferred approach.

💞

Couples Counselling

Rebuild trust and communication using emotionally focused and evidence-based methods — for couples at any stage, from new to decades-long.

🏡

Family Therapy

A guided space for families to work through conflict, major transitions, or communication breakdowns together.

🌱

Teen & Adolescent Counselling

Specialised support for ages 12–19 navigating identity, school stress, friendships, and emotional regulation.

🌊

Anxiety & Stress Management

Practical, CBT-based tools to manage anxiety, panic, and chronic stress, alongside deeper work on root causes.

🌤️

Depression Support

Compassionate, structured support to help you regain energy, motivation, and a sense of self.

🕊️

Grief & Loss Counselling

A gentle space to process loss — of a loved one, a relationship, or a former version of life — at your own pace.

🛡️

Trauma-Informed Therapy

Safe, paced support for processing past experiences, using trauma-informed and somatic-aware techniques.

💻

Online Counselling

Full access to all of the above via secure video sessions — for clients who prefer or need to meet remotely.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept insurance?

We work with several major insurance providers and offer sliding-scale rates for clients paying out of pocket. Contact us for details specific to your plan.

How long is a typical session?

Individual sessions are 50 minutes; couples and family sessions are typically 60–75 minutes.

How do I know which service is right for me?

Book a free 15-minute consultation — we'll talk through what you're looking for and recommend the best fit.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. All sessions are strictly confidential, in line with professional and legal standards, with narrow exceptions required by law.

Not sure where to start?

Book a free consultation and we'll help you find the right service.
